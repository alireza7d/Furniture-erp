Compliance

Operating with integrity and in accordance with international standards.

At PV Tech LLC, compliance is fundamental to how we conduct business. We are committed to operating transparently and in full accordance with applicable international laws and regulations governing maritime trade.

🌐

International Regulations

We adhere to all applicable international maritime regulations and trade laws. Our operations are conducted in compliance with the rules set forth by relevant maritime authorities and international trade bodies.

🛡

Sanctions Compliance

PV Tech LLC maintains strict sanctions compliance procedures. We screen all counterparties, vessels, and transactions against applicable sanctions lists including those maintained by OFAC, EU, and UN. We do not engage in any trade that violates international sanctions regimes.

🔍

KYC & Due Diligence

We conduct thorough Know Your Customer (KYC) and due diligence checks on all business partners and counterparties before entering into any commercial arrangement. This includes verification of corporate identity, beneficial ownership, and trading history.

⚖

Ethical Business Practices

We are committed to conducting all business activities ethically and with integrity. PV Tech LLC maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward bribery, corruption, and any form of fraudulent business conduct. We expect the same standards from our business partners.

Our Commitment

Compliance is not merely a regulatory obligation for PV Tech LLC — it is a core value. We continuously review and update our internal procedures to ensure they meet evolving international standards. We believe that responsible business practices are essential for building long-term relationships in the maritime industry.

If you have any compliance-related questions or require documentation regarding our procedures, please do not hesitate to contact us.
