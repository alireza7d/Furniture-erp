About Us

An independent maritime trading and chartering company.

Who We Are

PV Tech LLC is an independent maritime trading and chartering company. We connect cargo owners with reliable vessel solutions, facilitating the movement of bulk commodities across international waters. Our team combines industry knowledge with a hands-on approach to every transaction.

What We Do

We specialize in dry bulk cargo transportation, offering chartering and brokerage services that cover the full lifecycle of a voyage — from initial cargo-vessel matching through to post-fixture coordination. Our focus is on delivering straightforward, professional service to both shipowners and charterers.

Markets We Operate In

Our operations span key maritime trade regions, with active involvement in routes covering:

Black Sea

Grain, coal, and mineral exports from major Black Sea ports.

Middle East

Bulk commodity imports and regional trade flows in the Arabian Gulf.

India

Coal, minerals, and agricultural cargo serving the Indian subcontinent.

Africa

Growing trade routes across East and West African ports.

Our Approach

We believe in clear communication, prompt responses, and honest dealings. In an industry built on trust, we aim to be a dependable counterpart for every transaction — regardless of size. We do not overstate our capabilities or make promises we cannot keep.

We welcome inquiries from shipowners, charterers, and cargo interests.
