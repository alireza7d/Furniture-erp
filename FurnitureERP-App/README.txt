========================================
  FurnitureERP - Furniture Management
========================================

HOW TO RUN:
  Double-click "Open FurnitureERP.bat"

  OR just double-click "FurnitureERP.html"

  Both will open the app in your browser.

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
  - Dashboard with KPIs
  - CRM Pipeline (Kanban board)
  - Sales Orders
  - Point of Sale
  - Inventory Management
  - Purchase Orders
  - Manufacturing
  - Accounting & Invoicing
  - Email & SMS Marketing
  - Contacts & Products

NOTE: This is a demo/preview version with sample data.
      Creating or editing records is disabled in this mode.
